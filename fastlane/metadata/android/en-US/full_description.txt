GateControl is the app for your own GateControl server – the self-hosted gateway for your home network or office. It connects your phone to your network over WireGuard® and brings your services, your smart home and remote access together in one place.

Requires your own GateControl server (self-hosted). The app itself collects no data and only talks to your server.

VPN
• WireGuard tunnel with one tap, also as a Quick Settings tile and widget
• Connect automatically, always-on VPN, automatic reconnect
• Trusted Wi-Fi: disconnects automatically at home, connects again on the go
• Split tunnelling by networks or apps
• DNS leak test, connection and traffic statistics

Services
• All services your server shares with you at a glance, with favourites and online status
• Open them right in the app – signed in automatically where your administrator allows it
• Remote desktop (RDP) with Wake-on-LAN

Quick actions and notifications
• Lights, air conditioning, your car and more from your server's plugins – on the start screen, as app shortcuts, tiles and a widget
• Built-in notification center without third-party push services: messages come straight from your server

Setup
• Scan the setup code or QR code from your server – done
• Settings can be managed centrally by your administrator

Privacy
• No tracking, no ads, no analytics SDKs
• Credentials encrypted on the device
• Support bundles and crash reports go – redacted – only to your own server
