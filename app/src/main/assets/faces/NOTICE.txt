Face models used by GateControl (Drive › Fotos › Personen), unchanged from OpenCV Zoo
(https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo). They run on the phone only.

YuNet — face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx
  https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
  Copyright (c) 2020 Shiqi Yu. MIT License (LICENSE-YuNet.txt).

SFace — face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx (loaded by the app when switched on, not part of the package)
  https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface
  Contributed by Yaoyao Zhong; ONNX conversion by Chengrui Wang.
