GateControl VPN Client

Privacy Policy

Overview

GateControl VPN Client is a self-hosted VPN management app. It connects exclusively to your own GateControl server. We do not operate any cloud infrastructure, relay servers, or data collection endpoints.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Specifically, the app does not:

  • Send analytics or telemetry to any third party
  • Track your browsing activity or app usage, or your location for anyone but your own server's family features (see below)
  • Display advertisements
  • Share data with advertising networks or data brokers
  • Store personal data on any server we control

Network Communication

All network traffic is directed exclusively to the GateControl server URL that you configure during setup. The app communicates with:

  • Your GateControl server — for VPN configuration, license validation, and service management
  • Your WireGuard endpoint — for encrypted VPN tunnel traffic

Profile picture and favourite colour — only if you set them (Settings › Profile): the picture you choose in the system photo picker (downscaled to 256 × 256 px) and your favourite colour are stored on your own GateControl server and are visible to the users of that server (apps, portal, wall tablet, admin). "Remove" deletes the picture on the server. The app keeps profile pictures from your server only in its cache.

No data is sent to any other destination.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to establish the VPN connection to your server
  • CAMERA — Used solely for scanning QR codes during initial server setup. Camera access is only activated when you explicitly open the QR scanner. No images or video are stored or transmitted.
  • VPN Service — Required by Android to create the WireGuard VPN tunnel
  • Boot Completed — Optional auto-connect on device startup (user-configurable)
  • Notifications — To display VPN connection status

Family Features (Familie plugin)

The Family tab only appears when your own GateControl server offers the "Familie" plugin and you are a member of the family. Shopping lists, tasks, dates, place reminders, rides, the emergency card, pocket money, wish lists, the household book, the pinboard and the meal plan are stored on your own server; shopping lists and the emergency card (encrypted) are also kept on the device so they work offline.

  • Locating children — a child's location is only determined when a parent of the family asks for it, once; there is no continuous tracking. Depending on the family setting the child's phone sends its precise location (coordinates, accuracy, time, battery level) back automatically — for this also in the background while the app is closed — or only after the child confirms. The child is always notified and sees who located it and when. Locating ends automatically when the child turns 18.
  • "I've arrived" and SOS — sent only when the user taps; an SOS includes the location if available.
  • Place reminders — the phone checks the chosen places itself with Android geofencing (Google Play services), also in the background. The location is not sent for this; only the fact that a reminder fired (without coordinates), and only when the reminder notifies someone. Reminders for other family members only run after they agree.
  • Pick-ups & rides — who picks up whom, when and where (a place name and, if chosen, its coordinates) is stored on your own server; asking "who can?" notifies the parents. Coordinates of a ride are only used for an optional place reminder on the driver's phone.
  • Emergency card — health data the family enters (allergies, medication, conditions, blood type, weight, health insurance, emergency numbers) is stored on your own server and readable by the members of the family. So it also works without a connection, the app keeps a copy of the last card on the phone, encrypted (AES-256-GCM with a key in the Android Keystore), excluded from backups and deleted when you leave the family or the feature is switched off. It is never shown in notifications or written to logs. The call buttons only open the phone's dialer.
  • Pocket money, wish lists, household book — amounts the family books are bookkeeping only: the app never pays, charges or transfers money and asks for no bank or card details. Who reserved a wish is never shown to the person who wished for it.
  • Presence ("who is home") — only if you switch on "Share presence" yourself (off by default, can be switched off at any time). The app then reports to your own server only "home" or "away", recognised by whether your phone is in the home network (the server's own network or the network behind one of its gateways); to check, the app asks your server and sends it your phone's local Wi-Fi / LAN address (e.g. 192.168.1.23) — no GPS, no coordinates, no Wi-Fi name. It reports a change after it held for two minutes, also in the background (on network changes and about every 15 minutes). The family sees it in the app, on the wall tablet and in the portal; parents can let smart-home rules react to it. Without your consent you are shown as "not shared" and nothing is sent.
  • Retention — the server deletes coordinates 7 days after the answer; the log of who asked when (without coordinates) is kept 90 days.
  • Maps — the map in the app loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), which learns the map area shown and your IP address, not whose location it is. Addresses come from the device's own geocoder if present.

None of this data is sent to us or to other third parties.

Data Storage

All configuration data (server URL, API token, WireGuard keys) is stored locally on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256). No credentials are stored in plain text. RDP credentials (Pro feature) use end-to-end encryption (ECDH + AES-256-GCM) and are never stored unencrypted on the device.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google ML Kit (on-device barcode scanning) for QR code recognition. ML Kit runs entirely on-device and does not transmit any data to Google servers. For family place reminders the app uses Google Play services geofencing on the device, and its maps load tiles from OpenStreetMap (see "Family Features").

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for operators and users of a GateControl server. Children only use it through the family features of their own family's server, set up by their parents or the server operator (see "Family Features"); no information about children is collected by us or third parties.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the server administrator of your GateControl instance.
